# VN Stock Sim — What It Does

VN Stock Sim is a simulation-first platform for analyzing, practicing, and backtesting trades on Vietnamese stocks (HOSE, HNX, UPCOM) — without risking real money.

**Core capabilities:**

- **Market data & charts** — Live/historical prices for VN-Index, VN30, HNX, UPCOM, and every listed stock, with candlestick charts and technical indicators (SMA, EMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger, and more).
- **Stock screener & heatmap** — Filter the market by fundamentals and technicals; see the whole market at a glance.
- **Strategy Builder** — Create trading rules visually, no coding required.
- **Backtesting** — Test any strategy against historical data and see return, win rate, drawdown, and profit factor.
- **Paper trading** — A virtual portfolio with real order types (market, limit, stop) and no real-money risk.
- **Replay Mode** — Step through historical price action candle by candle and trade blind to the future, then get scored — the platform's signature feature: *"Trade the past before you trade the future."*
- **Trade Journal** — Every simulated trade logged automatically, with performance analytics over time.
- **AI Assistant (Quant)** — Natural-language stock screening and strategy generation (later phase).

**Who it's for:** Vietnamese individual investors, students learning technical analysis, quant/trading-system developers, and anyone who wants to practice trading before using real capital.
